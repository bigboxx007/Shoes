Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our footwear services, doorstep trials, and restoration.

How does the Tricity At-Home "Try & Buy" trial work?
Simply pick up to 3 pairs or different sizes on our website or via WhatsApp. Our delivery associate brings them to your home in Chandigarh, Mohali, or Panchkula. You can try them on clean indoor carpets, keep the pair that fits like a dream, and hand the others back immediately with zero return fees or advance payment.
Can you clean non-Harsh shoes at the Shoe Spa?
Yes! While our enzyme formulas were engineered specifically for natural Merino wool and bio-knits, our Shoe Spa expertly services luxury sneakers, leather derbies, running shoes, and knit footwear from all brands.
How does the ₹500 Old Shoe Recycling Trade-In work?
Bring any pair of worn-out shoes (any brand, any condition) to our Flagship Studio at SCO 65-66, Sector 34B, Chandigarh. You will receive an instant ₹500 discount voucher to redeem on any Harsh Walkwear pair. The old shoes are stripped into rubber granules and recycled into playground safety surfaces.
How long does the Shoe Spa treatment take?
Express Refresh is completed in 24 hours. Deep Eco-Spa and Sole Un-yellowing treatments take 48 to 72 hours to allow complete gentle natural air-drying and bonding of the nano-stain shield.
Is the 3D Foot Gait Analysis really free?
Yes, 100% complimentary! Whether you are walking in to test our shoes or booking a slot online, we believe everyone deserves to know their true arch profile and pressure distribution without paying consultation charges.
How do I order custom monogrammed pairs for weddings or corporate gifts?
Choose "Corporate & Wedding Suite" in our booking form or message Harsh directly at +91 6230427856. We will provide digital mockups with your initials or company logo, custom metallic aglets, and arrange a private size-fitting session.
